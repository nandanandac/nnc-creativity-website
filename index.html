Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Our Design Services

Helpful details about ordering graphic design, packaging and Photoshop editing from NNC Creativity.

What graphic design services does NNC Creativity provide?

We provide product label design, food and beverage packaging, stickers, boxes and pouches, cosmetic labels, logo and branding, packaging mockups, print-ready artwork, posters, background removal and Photoshop photo editing.

Can I order design services from outside Bengaluru?

Yes. NNC Creativity is based in Rajajinagar, Bengaluru, and works remotely with individuals and businesses across India. You can share your requirements and review designs online.

What are your design package prices?

Our listed starting packages are ₹499 for a single label or sticker design, ₹1,499 for a packaging design and ₹2,499 for a brand-launch package. Final pricing may vary with the project scope and complexity.

How many revisions are included?

The Basic and Standard packages include 2 revisions each. The Premium Brand Launch package includes 3 revisions. Additional changes or extra variants can be discussed separately.

Do you provide printing?

Our service focuses on design and artwork. Print-ready files can be provided as included in the selected package. Printing, extra variants and complex artwork are quoted separately.

How can I start a design project?

Share your product details, required size, text, preferred style and any reference images through email or WhatsApp. We can discuss the suitable design package and next steps.
